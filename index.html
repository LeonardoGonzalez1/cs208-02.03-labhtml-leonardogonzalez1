Caldwell, Idaho

Location

Caldwell, Idaho is located in southwestern Idaho, about 30 miles west of Boise. It is part of the Treasure Valley along the Boise River. The city is known for its agricultural surroundings and its proximity to Boise and Oregon.

About Caldwell

Attractions

There are many places to go in Caldwell. For example, the Indian Creek Plaza in downtown Caldwell. It hosts community events, markets, seasonal activities, and has many stores with things to do.

Activities

There is lots of opportunity to get to know the city. Events like a fun night out at the Caldwell Night Rodeo, hiking to explore the scenery of the Sunnyslope Wine Region, and swimming or fishing at Lake Lowell.

Population

The population of Caldwell has been rising since 2020. The current population is around 77,000 people. People are drawn by its appeal as part of the Treasure Valley and the expanding neighborhoods of Caldwell.
